a tiny generative art toy

doodly

Every face here is grown from a single number. Pick a seed and the math wobbles some ink across sketchbook paper: heads, hair, hats, moods. No two pages alike, every page repeatable.

↑ real faces, sketched live in your browser just now

  • seeded & shareable: the URL carries the seed; send a friend your exact page.
  • heads that turn: open any doodle and drag to spin it around a real 3D skull.
  • they watch you: enlarge a face and its pupils follow your mouse.
  • yours to keep: export the sheet as PNG, or any face as crisp vector SVG.
